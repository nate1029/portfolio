01 — why

Why I build

the belief underneath everything below.

I trained as an electronics engineer, and it rewired how I see the world: everything is a system — inputs, flows, feedback. Most people trade hours for output. Systems don't. So I decided early that I'd rather spend my hours building things that keep producing after I stop: a circuit, an app, a storefront, an episode.

  • № 1Code works while you sleep.
  • № 2Media compounds — record once, broadcast forever.
  • № 3Machines don't get tired. People shouldn't have to.
02 — how

How I work

systems thinking, one person, end to end.

step 1

Map the system

Every business is a circuit: inputs, flows, bottlenecks. Before anything gets built, I find where the signal is being lost.

step 2

Build end to end

One brief, one builder — scoping, design, code, launch. No hand-offs, no telephone game, no "that's another team".

step 3

Automate, then broadcast

Make it run itself, then tell the story. The build creates value; the broadcast multiplies it.
